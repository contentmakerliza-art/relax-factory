Relax Factory Uploader

A private, single-user tool of an independent YouTube creator.

Uploads to YouTube · uses YouTube API Services

Relax Factory is a small web app on my own server. I use it to render long ambient videos (cozy cabin scenes with fireplace, rain and snow sounds) from clips and sounds I make myself, and to upload the finished videos to my own YouTube channels.

What it does with YouTube

After I connect one of my channels with Google sign-in (OAuth), the app uploads a finished video to that channel with its title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time, and adds it to a playlist. Files are 20–40 GB, too large to upload from a home connection, so the upload runs from the server.

Who can use it

Only me. The app is protected by a password and is not offered to anyone else. It only works with channels I own.

Data

The app does not collect, store or share any data about viewers or other people. See the privacy policy.
