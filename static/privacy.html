Privacy Policy

Relax Factory Uploader · last updated 23 October 2026

Relax Factory Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to upload her own videos to her own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Google gives the app an access token for that channel. The app uses it only to read the channel's name and ID, upload videos, set their details and thumbnails, and add them to the channel's playlists.

What data the app stores

For each connected channel the app stores the channel's ID, its name and a refresh token. They are kept only on the app's server, in a folder readable only by the app, and are never shared with anyone or sent anywhere except to Google to call the YouTube API. The app does not collect, store or share any data about viewers, commenters or any other people, uses no cookies for tracking and shows no ads.

How to remove access and data

Pressing "Disconnect" next to a channel in the app revokes its token with Google and deletes the stored data for that channel at once. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services → Relax Factory Uploader → Delete all connections). When the app next finds that access was revoked this way, it deletes the stored data for that channel.
